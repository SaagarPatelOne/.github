# Code of Conduct

Projects in the `SaagarPatelOne` organization should be respectful, constructive, and professional.

Expected behavior:

- Be kind, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent, but communicate clearly.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and behavior, not on people.
- Share context, reproduction steps, and evidence when raising problems.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure norms.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning language
- Repeated low-effort spam or disruptive behavior
- Publicly disclosing active vulnerabilities without a coordinated path

Moderation is handled by the repository owner. Reports can be sent to `saagarpatel08@gmail.com`.
